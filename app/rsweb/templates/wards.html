Wards & hubs

A system ward stops other players mining in its system; a system hub does too, and relays, speeds travel and shields itself for {{ 7 }} days after activation, then loses 10 % capacity a day unless it's maintained. Wards and hubs don't share a system, and an account may run at most {{ w.cap }} wards.

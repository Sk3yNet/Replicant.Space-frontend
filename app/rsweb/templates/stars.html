The game's star catalogue only covers about 70 ly around Sol. Further out, a stellar census from a heaven or cargo vessel lists the stars around it: positions, entry points and whether anyone has explored them. They're added to the catalogue here, so the map, routes and travel know them. The Stellar census on arrival rule runs one in every new system.

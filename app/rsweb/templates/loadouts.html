Phases

A phase is a stage of a system's development, and how many of each device a system at that stage should have. Every device counted for a system gets a home:<system> tag, so a carrier or transport out on a delivery still counts at home (listed under "Away from home") and isn't made spare where it's visiting. A device marked spare loses its home tag — it belongs to no system until it's sent somewhere (or its own system needs it again). Delivered devices are re-homed on arrival; to move one for good, change its tag on its device page. Leave a box empty for "don't care" (never counted, marked spare or filled); 0 means none wanted, so any there become spare.

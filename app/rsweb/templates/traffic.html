Civilisation contact

A civilisation only sends follow-up requests (the daily messages about new events) when an FTL beacon is deployed at the planet or moon of one of its events. A beacon in the system's Kuiper belt or Oort cloud logs traffic but doesn't count for this. The rule places one as soon as a survey discovers an event, so it's already there when you complete it.

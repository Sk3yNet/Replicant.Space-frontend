Relay network

Your working FTL relays (7.5 ly, or the range a relay reports) and active system hubs (15 ly) link when they're within reach of each other; linked ones form a network, each in its own color here and on the galaxy map (relay network option). Other players' relays don't join yours. A relay must sit at an L4/L5 point; it may go in a warded system. A network is active when one of your replicants is within its reach: only then can devices it covers be commanded. Anything delivered beyond an active network rides a vessel hosting a replicant, or isn't sent.

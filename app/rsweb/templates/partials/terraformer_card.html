Assignment and steering

The fleet waits for its whole loadout and an active replicant aboard (it works outside relay coverage), flies to the planet with everyone, deploys its terraform devices there and steers them from the monitor's reports: each value outside its band gets the devices that move it the right way, harder the farther it is, until it nears the middle of the range. Strong heaters and coolers only for big gaps; bio seeders once their preconditions hold; nothing pushes into an active disaster. Once temperature, pressure and hydrosphere are in range, the atmospheric regulator locks them while oxygen, toxicity and biosphere are finished. It stays and reports terraforming for as long as it is assigned, holding the world in range, until you give it another planet or recall it. Disasters and anomalies notify you.

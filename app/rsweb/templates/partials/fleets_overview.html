Materials: a fleet set to send to another fleet has its home system's biggest stockpile ferried to that fleet's home by cargo freighters (surge drive) under a dedicated AMI transport controller there, the ferry controller (tagged ferry). A fleet that takes materials in is a destination: mining missions in deliver mode ferry to the one they send to, else the nearest such fleet, and the spare depot defaults to one of them. Deliveries land at the home system's autofactory, else its biggest stockpile, else its entry point. One route per system.

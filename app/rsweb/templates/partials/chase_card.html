Target criteria

How a target is picked: of the asteroids in flight that the app knows of (a hub or the fleet's own launch found them) in the search area, the fleet keeps those at least min size, with a resource still to mine, and with at least min hours of mining left once it has flown there and before it must leave. It prefers a rock in the system it is in, then the first resource on the priority list, then the most mining hours. A rock another chaser fleet already works is never taken; a dropped rock or a failed trip is skipped for 6 h.

Logistics

Your logistics fleets (Fleets: role logistics, filled to their loadout like any fleet) carry materials on demand: what queued prints need, contracts you approved or whose civilization you pre-approved (Contracts page), and trades you approved (Shop page; once each — plain shop listings are never fulfilled). Priority: queued prints (waiting ones first), local deliveries, long-range transfers, stray stockpiles back to a gather point, then rescues of devices out of command range. Home systems keep what their own prints need. Outside an active relay network a logistics device hosting a replicant flies alongside, or nothing goes; afterwards the logistics devices wait inside the network. Devices go the same way: deliveries take logistics carriers first.

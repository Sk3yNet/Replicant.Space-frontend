Logistics

Your logistics fleets (Fleets: role logistics, filled to their loadout like any fleet) carry materials on demand: what queued prints need, contracts you approved or whose civilization you pre-approved (Contracts page), and trades you approved (Shop page; once each — plain shop listings are never fulfilled). Priority: queued prints (waiting ones first), local deliveries, long-range transfers, stray stockpiles back to a gather point, then rescues of devices out of command range, then repair patrols (the logistics fleets’ repair devices to the most worn systems). Home systems keep what their own prints need. Each request has a task category (prints, trades & contracts, rescues, shop listings, repair patrols): every free carrier is given one category that has work and keeps it until that category runs dry, so small categories aren’t starved by big ones. Evacuations go first with every carrier; carriers left over then help any category, stockpile returns last. Outside an active relay network a logistics device hosting a replicant flies alongside, or nothing goes; afterwards the logistics devices wait inside the network. Devices go the same way: deliveries take logistics carriers first.

Reset & reform

When the tags have got messy: wipe the assignment tags and rebuild every fleet and controller assignment from where the devices actually are. Nothing is sent until you press Apply, and the preview lists every change first.

What it does

  1. Mobile fleets (not on a mission) keep their members up to their loadout, then take unassigned devices (spare or untagged) in the system they're in. Extra members leave the fleet.
  2. Each system's home fleet takes the devices in that system, up to its template — devices already tagged home: there first. Types the template leaves blank stay home.
  3. Everything else in a system with a home fleet becomes spare. Systems without one are left alone (devices that were spare there stay spare).
  4. Controllers: a drone run by a controller outside its new group is released; the next loadout pass and the fleets' own phases adopt it again.

What it doesn't touch

Rewritten: home: to: fleet: spare gather. Kept: at: pins, civ, ferry, taxi, ignore tags and your own tags.

Left alone: ignored devices, the ferry and its freighters/plates, fleets on a mission, devices in a running job, anything moving or in transit (a delivery in flight keeps its to: tag), and the replicants' vessels.

Turn the loadout rule off first if you don't want a pass to start moving spares straight after.

Reset & reform

When the tags have got messy: wipe the assignment tags and rebuild every fleet and controller assignment from where the devices actually are. Nothing is sent until you press Apply, and the preview lists every change first.

What it does

  1. Fleets that aren't stationed (and not on a mission) keep their members up to their loadout, then take unassigned devices (spare or untagged) in the system they're in. Extra members leave the fleet.
  2. Each stationed fleet takes the devices in its home system up to its loadout: its own members first, then devices with an old home: tag there, then the rest.
  3. Everything else in a system with a stationed fleet becomes spare if a fleet there counts its type, and stays untagged if none does. Systems without one are left alone (devices that were spare there stay spare).
  4. Controllers: a drone run by a controller outside its new group is released; the next loadout pass and the fleets' own phases adopt it again. A stationed fleet's devices and the untagged ones in its home are one group.

What it doesn't touch

Rewritten: fleet: to: spare gather and old home: tags. Kept: at: pins, civ, ferry, taxi, ignore tags and your own tags.

Left alone: ignored devices, the ferry and its freighters/plates, fleets away on a mission, devices in a running job, anything moving or in transit (a delivery in flight keeps its to: tag), and the replicants' vessels.

Turn the loadout rule off first if you don't want a pass to start moving spares straight after.

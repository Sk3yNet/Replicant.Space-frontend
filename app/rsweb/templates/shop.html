Trade shop

A shop is an AMI trade controller with the trade directive. With an active FTL relay (or system hub) in its system it's listed in the galactic directory; without one it trades locally only. A trade's rewards go into escrow when you create it — you need them at the shop's location for the whole stock — and come back if you remove it.

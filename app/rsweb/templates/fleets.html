A fleet is a named group of devices, tagged fleet:<id>, with a home system and a loadout (its own lines, or a template). A stationed fleet is kept at its loadout in its home system: fleetless devices there join it, extras become spare, spares are sent to it and what's still missing is printed. Its devices at home are that system's own, so the in-system rules (AMI schedules, idle miners, salvage …) work them, and mining and explore missions can't target its home. Any fleet can go on a mission; while it's away the rules leave its devices alone, and when it's done the loadout pass takes over again. Tags in a mess? Reset & reform.

Templates (phases)

A template (phase) is a loadout fleets can share, e.g. a stage of a system's development: how many of each device a fleet on it should have. Editing a template updates every fleet that follows it. A fleet's members keep their fleet: tag wherever they go, so a carrier out on a delivery still counts at home (listed under "Away from home") and isn't made spare where it's visiting. A device marked spare is in no fleet until it's sent to one. Leave a box empty for "don't care" (never counted, marked spare or filled); 0 means none wanted, so a stationed fleet makes any spare. The second box (+) is keep in stock: that many more are kept as spares at the fleet's home, printed one at a time on an autofactory there with nothing else to print (supplied by logistics last, queued behind every other print), for any fleet or ward / relay order to take.

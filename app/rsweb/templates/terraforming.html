Dropping rocks {{ R.flying }} in flight · {{ R.systems | sum(attribute='charges') }} vector charge(s)

Detect a Kuiper object, launch it with a vector charge deployed at that Kuiper belt, then steer it. A sensor array's detect takes about an hour and found objects stay 24 h. Launch picks a planet or moon in the same system. Propulsors change speed and ETA (increase / decrease); trajectory deflectors steepen the angle (above 30° hits the surface) or shallow it (spreads it through the air). Rocks you launch are yours: asteroid defense leaves them alone, but devices there are still evacuated. A rock chaser fleet (Fleets) does all of this by itself.

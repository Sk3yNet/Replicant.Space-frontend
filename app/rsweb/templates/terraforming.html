Dropping rocks

Find a Kuiper object (detect_object on a sensor array, or a vessel the slow way; found ones stay 24 h), deploy a vector charge at it and launch it at a planet or moon in the same system. Then steer it with propulsors (increase / decrease: speed and ETA) and trajectory deflectors (steepen above 30° to hit the surface, shallow to spread it through the air). Rocks you launch here are yours: asteroid defense leaves them alone (they still get evacuated around).

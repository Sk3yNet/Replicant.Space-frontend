Diagnostics

Read-only: about 60 GET requests at most, on the background budget, so automations and polling keep working. It reads every device, stockpiles, the mining controllers, the belts your miners are at, recent mining/AMI events and the logs of idle drones, and bundles them with the app's own rule settings, jobs and log. Your API token is never included. Download the JSON and attach it in the chat to test against live data.
